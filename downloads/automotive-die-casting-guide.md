# Automotive Die Casting Guide: Specifying Structural Castings

Copy-pasteable specification checklist for automotive structural die castings (shock towers, rails, cross-members, door inners).

## 1. Quality System Requirements
- [ ] **IATF 16949 certified at the producing site** — non-negotiable for automotive structural supply.
- [ ] **Full APQP with PPAP (18 elements)** — PSW sign-off, capacity study, process FMEA at gate reviews before series.
- [ ] **Cpk ≥ 1.33 critical / ≥ 1.67 safety** characteristics — structural joints are safety items.

## 2. Process Selection: Vacuum HPDC
- [ ] **Vacuum-assisted HPDC for weldable/heat-treatable parts** — removes entrained gas so T7 and cosmetic welding don't blister.
- [ ] **Verify porosity by X-ray** against ASTM E505 / NADCA reference at the specified vacuum level.
- [ ] **Confirm gating and thermal balance** — vacuum cannot fix a poor gating design.

## 3. Metallurgy and Heat Treatment
- [ ] **Specify T7 heat treatment** — as-cast tempers are too brittle for energy absorption.
- [ ] **Elongation > 8–12% after T7** — ductility (not just strength) drives crash energy absorption.
- [ ] **Transverse tensile coupons from production castings**, tested per ASTM B557 / equivalent.
- [ ] **Alloy matched to method and properties** — see aluminium die-casting alloy data.

## 4. Geometry for Crash Load Paths
- [ ] **Define crash load paths** on the print and in CAE sign-off — caster places material where energy is absorbed.
- [ ] **Local wall thickening at attachment/crush zones** with filleted transitions.
- [ ] **Avoid stress-raising sharp corners** in load paths; use generous radii.

## 5. Joining and Corrosion Isolation
- [ ] **Al-on-steel corrosion isolation** — insulated spacers, sealants, or coated fasteners; validate in corrosion cycling.
- [ ] **Weld/adhesive bond prep defined** for MIG, rivet-bond, or structural adhesive — only vacuum HPDC gives weldable surfaces.
- [ ] **Coating/pretreatment compatible** with the assembly paint process.

## 6. Inspection and Acceptance
- [ ] **Porosity acceptance by X-ray** (ASTM E505 / NADCA).
- [ ] **Leak and dimensional checks** against control plan; CMM on datums.
- [ ] **Traceability** of alloy lot, furnace, and machine per part/batch.

## Reference Standards
- IATF 16949:2016; AIAG APQP/PPAP (18 elements; Cpk 1.33/1.67)
- ASTM E505 (radiographs); ASTM B557 (tension); NADCA structural casting standards
