# OEM Supplier Audit Checklist for Casting and Machined Parts

Copy-pasteable audit checklist for sourcing casting and CNC machining suppliers. Print or paste into your QMS. Cover QSA and QPA at every awarded site.

## 1. Audit Structure: QSA vs QPA
- [ ] **Run a QSA (Quality System Audit)** against IATF 16949 / ISO 9001 — confirms documented, repeatable processes, not firefighting.
- [ ] **Run a QPA (Process/Product Audit)** on the live line, tooling, and parts — a good QMS on paper can still ship bad parts.
- [ ] **Block PPAP if QSA passes but QPA fails** — close process gaps before production-part approval.

## 2. Quality Certifications
- [ ] **Valid IATF 16949 certificate** from an accredited body — baseline for automotive casting/machining supply.
- [ ] **ISO 9001** for non-automotive or standalone secondary ops — not a substitute for IATF 16949 on automotive programs.
- [ ] **Certificate scope covers the audited site** — the casting/machining processes at the visited plant must be in scope, not a sister facility.

## 3. Equipment and Clamp Tonnage
- [ ] **Machine tonnage covers required projected area** — required clamp force ≈ projected area × specific pressure (≈40–90 MPa alu HPDC). Undersized = flash/short fill.
- [ ] **Tonnage class matches part** — general HPDC ~150–3500 t; structural/mega-castings need 2000–6000 t class.
- [ ] **Machine condition verified** — tie-bar stretch calibration, platen parallelism logs, preventive maintenance records.

## 4. In-House vs Outsourced Operations
- [ ] **CNC machining in-house** — better process control and traceability than sub-contracted finishing; confirm critical features are not silently outsourced.
- [ ] **Surface treatment/plating** — if outsourced, require sub-supplier approval and control-plan flow-down plus valid environmental permits.
- [ ] **Leak test (housings)** — in-house pressure-decay or helium; method, limit, and frequency in the control plan.
- [ ] **X-ray / CT (NDT)** — confirm NDT level, equipment, and inspection frequency, in-house or contracted.

## 5. Measurement and Inspection
- [ ] **CMM + ISO/IEC 17025 accredited lab** for material/mechanical testing — makes reported Cpk and tensile results trustworthy.
- [ ] **Active SPC on critical characteristics** — Cpk ≥ 1.33 critical / ≥ 1.67 safety; ask for live charts, not a policy.
- [ ] **Calibration system** — gauges calibrated on schedule, traceable to national standards; gauge R&R on key features.

## 6. Problem Solving and CAPA
- [ ] **8D maturity** — review 3 recent 8Ds for structured root cause (5 Why / Ishikawa) and verified effectiveness.
- [ ] **CAPA closure rate and on-time delivery** of corrective actions.
- [ ] **Lessons-learned linked to PFMEA and control plan** — closed loop prevents recurrence.

## 7. Site, Safety, and Financial Health
- [ ] **5S and housekeeping** on the shop floor.
- [ ] **Molten-metal safety** — PPE, melt-house guarding, pyrotechnic/explosion controls, furnace venting.
- [ ] **Financial health** — years in business, customer concentration, capacity utilization.
- [ ] **Business continuity plan** — melt disruption, key-machine breakdown, logistics.

## Audit Summary
| Area | Pass criterion |
| --- | --- |
| QSA | IATF 16949 valid; scope matches site |
| QPA | Cpk ≥1.33 crit / ≥1.67 safety; live SPC |
| Equipment | Lock tonnage ≥ required; maintained |
| In-house ops | Machining/leak-test in control; subs approved |
| Inspection | ISO 17025 lab; calibrated CMM; R&R |
| CAPA | 8D with verified root cause; on-time |
| Site/safety | 5S; molten-metal controls; BCP |
