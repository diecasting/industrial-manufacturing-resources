Casting Defect Catalog: Causes, Identification and Correction

Overview

Most die casting rejects trace back to a handful of defect types. Correcting them starts with identifying which one you have, because the same visible symptom can come from opposite causes — a void can be gas porosity (fix the fill) or shrinkage porosity (fix the feeding). This catalog lists the defects most frequently found in aluminum, zinc and magnesium HPDC, with the cause, how to recognize it, and the correction that addresses the root cause rather than the symptom.

The table is the working reference. Pair it with process control guidance in Die Casting Porosity Prevention and Aluminum Die Casting Process Guide.

Defect Catalog

DefectCauseIdentificationCorrection
Cold shutLow pour/die temperature or slow fill; two flow fronts meet without fusingVisible line or shallow crack on surface, often far from gateRaise melt and die temperature, increase fill rate, improve venting
MisrunInsufficient metal or fill velocity; thin section freezes before completeIncomplete part, short fill at extremities and ribsIncrease wall/runner size, raise velocity, preheat die
Porosity (gas)Air or moisture entrapment, turbulent fill, excess lubricant gasRound, smooth-walled voids; porous fracture surfaceReduce entrainment, vacuum assist, control lube, degas melt
Porosity (shrinkage)Localized shrinkage away from feeding path in thick sectionsIrregular, dendritic voids at thick sectionsAdd feeders, chill thick areas, balance wall thickness
Hot tearConstraint during solidification; high-shrinkage alloy at rigid featureCrack at corners or rib bases near the gateIncrease draft, round corners, reduce die constraint
Solder (die)Alloy welds to die steel at gate or hot spotMetal stuck to die, rough local surface transferAdjust die temperature, apply coating, reduce local heat
FlashExcess metal at parting line; low clamp force or worn shutoffThin fin of metal at the die split lineRaise clamp tonnage, maintain shutoff, reduce specific pressure
BlisterSubsurface gas expands on heating during plating or paint bakeDomed bubbles after coating or heat treatmentReduce porosity, lower bake temperature, degas melt
WarpageUneven cooling, residual stress, asymmetric wall sectionsDimensional distortion after ejectionBalance walls and cooling, redistribute ribbing, stress relieve
InclusionOxide, silicon or foreign particle carried in the meltHard spot or void with embedded particle, machining defectImprove melt handling, filtration, degassing
ErosionHigh-velocity metal washes and erodes die steel at gateGouged cavity near gate, progressive dimensional growthHarden cavity steel, tune velocity, balance gating

How to use this reference

  1. Section the suspect part and inspect the fracture: round voids = gas, dendritic voids = shrinkage.
  2. Match the symptom to the table, then apply the correction to the root cause — do not just raise pressure, which can worsen erosion and solder.
  3. For porosity specifically, separate gas from shrinkage before acting; the fixes are different. See Die Casting Porosity Prevention.
  4. Track defect type by cavity and shot to separate tool wear (erosion, solder, flash) from process drift (cold shut, misrun).
  5. Confirm alloy-related tendencies with Aluminum Die Casting Alloys and Zinc Die Casting Alloys.
